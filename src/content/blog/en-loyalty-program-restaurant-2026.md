Every restaurant owner wants regulars. But not all loyalty programs are created equal. Some collect dust. Others generate real revenue. Here's an honest breakdown.

## The Paper Loyalty Card: Simple but Limited

The classic punch card — "10 coffees, 1 free" — still works for simple habits. But it has real limitations:

- **Lost or forgotten** by most customers before they reach the reward
- **No data** collected on your customers
- **Easy to cheat** (self-punching, lost cards replaced without verification)
- **No digital reach** — you can't follow up after the visit

Best for: bakeries, coffee shops with very high repeat frequency. Not ideal for restaurants.

## Mobile Apps: Powerful but Expensive

Branded apps (like Starbucks Rewards) are the gold standard — but they require significant investment:

- €10,000–50,000+ to develop
- Ongoing maintenance and updates
- **Low adoption**: most customers won't download an app for a single restaurant

This works for large chains. For independent restaurants, it's overkill.

## Email/SMS Programs: Effective with the Right Setup

Collecting customer contact info and sending targeted campaigns (birthday offers, slow-day promotions) is one of the best ROI strategies. The challenge is **collection** — getting customers to sign up in the first place.

This is where a physical touchpoint (QR code, tablet, spin wheel) makes all the difference.

## Gamification: The Highest Conversion Format

Gamified loyalty — spin-the-wheel, instant wins, scratch cards — consistently outperforms traditional formats because it:

- Creates an **emotional moment** (excitement, anticipation)
- Gives an **immediate reward** — no waiting for a punch card to fill up
- **Collects customer data** automatically (email, phone)
- Triggers a **review request** at peak satisfaction
